## Overview

There are **311 Atlas passive points** to unlock in total. Expect ~30 hours of playtime to fully complete it. There are **two independent progression paths** — both matter, but start with Path 1:

| Path | What it is | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Main Atlas Points** | Oriath quest line + Arbiter bosses | Do first — applies to every map |
| **League Mechanics** | Abyss, Breach, Delirium, Ritual, etc. | Do in parallel after first Arbiter of Divinity kill |

> **Key principle:** Main Atlas points apply to *all* maps, so finishing this first amplifies everything else — more rare monsters, duplicated monsters, stronger tablet effects, etc.

## Path 1 — Main Atlas Points (Oriath Quest Line)

### Phase 1: The City

1. Enter maps → a **Precursor Tower** immediately appears — rush to it and complete it.
2. The tower unlocks a **large city** (Oriath). This is where all your main Atlas points come from:
   - Each **map** inside the city = **+1 Atlas point**
   - Each **precursor tower/wall** inside the city = **+3 Atlas points**
   - The city has 100–150+ maps total
3. Follow the quest line through the city — **do not try to complete every map yet**, just follow the main path.

### Phase 2: Arbiter of Ash

4. The quest line leads to the **Burning Monolith**, which gives two quests:
   - Go to the **Western Gateway** → enter and complete the **Western Enigma Chamber** (a Citadel-style boss fight).
   - Go to the **Eastern Gateway** → enter and complete the **Eastern Enigma Chamber** (same style).
5. Return to the **Burning Monolith** after both are done.
6. Fight the **Arbiter of Ash** — the first main pinnacle boss.

### Phase 3: Arbiter of Divinity

7. Killing the Arbiter of Ash **unlocks a new area**. Rush to the **Origin Tower** there.
8. Origin Tower gives a quest to **collect sparks** — go to:
   - **Patriarch Halls**
   - **Matriarch Halls**
   Kill both bosses.
9. Fight the **Arbiter of Divinity** — the major endgame boss. Unlimited attempts, so gear up but don't stress.

### Phase 4: Repeat Kills (The Grind)

After the first kill, **five stone statues** appear around the Arbiter of Divinity. Each statue **instantly completes all maps in one of the five Atlas regions** and awards all those Atlas points — this is the fast track to avoid clearing 150 maps manually.

**You must kill Arbiter of Divinity 5 times total** to unlock all five regions.

**Two ways to trigger the next fight:**

| Option | How | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Complete every map in the city | 150+ maps, one by one | Slow — not recommended |
| Find Patriarch & Matriarch Halls outside the city | Look like Citadels with a visible beam; kill 2 of them to trigger the fight | **Recommended** |

- Halls outside the city work **exactly like the city fight** — just kill the two hall bosses, then fight the Arbiter of Divinity again.
- You need **4 Patriarch Halls + 4 Matriarch Halls** total across the four extra kills.
- While searching for halls you also explore the Atlas, find Citadels, unique maps, Jade Islands, corrupted Nexuses, etc.

## Path 2 — League Mechanic Quests

League mechanics each have their own dedicated Atlas area and passive point pool. **You only need to do the ones you want to farm.**

**How it works:**
1. Complete a league mechanic **once inside any map** (e.g., run one Abyss crack).
2. That mechanic's **dedicated area is revealed** on the Atlas.
3. Go there and **complete all maps in that area** to earn every passive point for that mechanic.

**Available mechanics:** Abyss, Breach, Delirium, Ritual, Expedition, and others.

> League mechanic bosses are **significantly easier** than Arbiter of Divinity.

**Recommended timing:** Kill Arbiter of Divinity once → pick **one league mechanic** you want to focus on → do that mechanic while searching outside the city for Patriarch/Matriarch Halls. This lets you earn currency and progress the main quest at the same time.

## Masters

There are **three Masters** — all of them are very impactful. Unlock them alongside normal progression.

| Master | How to Unlock | How to Complete |
|---|---|---|
| **Doryani** | Complete one **corrupted nexus** | Do 3 more corrupted nexuses + kill the nexus boss (Fury something) |
| **Kirac** | Complete one **anomaly map** (e.g., Jade Island) | Complete 4 anomaly maps total (listed in the quest log) |
| **Hilda** | She just **appears on the Atlas** | Travel to her location and interact |

> Check your quest log for the exact map names required for each master — they are listed there.

## Atlas Tree Tips

You unlock **all 311 points** eventually, so early choices aren't permanent. That said, invest smartly:

**First points — prioritise "natural juice":**
- All **rare monster** nodes (increased rare monsters, rare modifiers) — strongest loot multiplier
- All **tablet** nodes (tablet quantity, tablet rarity, tablet effect) — amplifies all content
- **Essence** and **Shrines** — solid early currency and utility
- **Partial Translation** — unlocks 4-mod rare tablets, very important

**What to skip early:**
- The city (middle) area nodes — you won't be farming inside the city if you're hunting halls outside, so grab these last

**Quick search trick:** In the Atlas tree, search `rare` and allocate everything → then search `tablet` and allocate everything. This covers the highest-impact general nodes efficiently.
