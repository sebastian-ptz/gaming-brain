## Why Ritual

- **A-Tier** (some sources rate it S-Tier for targeted farming)
- **Only source of Omens** in the game — makes it permanently relevant
- 0.5 rework: **every reward is a Unique or Omen** — no filler at all
- GGG buffed dozens of previously worthless Uniques → market demand is high
- **Mod-stealing Rune system** — extract a modifier from Kalguuran/Ezomyte Uniques, seal into a Rune, socket into any weapon → early-league unique prices surge
- Self-funds your own crafting (Omens you farm go directly into your weapon/gear crafts)

---
## How It Works

1. Enter a map with Ritual altars (from Ritual Tablets on towers or Caer Tarth area)
2. Kill enemies inside the Ritual circle to reach the activation threshold
3. Activate the altar → enemies **revive and carry over** to the next altar
4. Complete **3–4 altars per map** → unlock the **Favours** shop
5. Spend **Tribute** (earned from altar kills) on Favours → Uniques and Omens

### Ritual Types
Five variants with different hazard effects: Contaminated, Infested, Tainted, Smothering, Sapping

### The Revive Penalty
> ⚠️ Monsters lose **25% Tribute value per revival**. The Atlas node **Reinvigorated Sacrifices** removes this entirely — a ~28% total Tribute increase on 4-altar maps. Take this early.

---
## Tribute Management

> 🔑 **Tribute is per-map — it's gone the moment you leave. Never waste it.**

### Priority Spend Order
1. **T0/T1 Uniques immediately** (Headhunter, Mageblood) — buy on sight
2. **High-value Omens** — see list below
3. **Defer expensive items** — costs 10% of Tribute, item returns later with 6% discount (compounds over runs)
4. **Reroll** low-value pages — costs 1,000 Tribute; with optimized tablets this can be free

### Keep ~1,000 Tribute in Reserve
Always hold back Tribute in case something valuable appears late in the shop window.

## Atlas Setup

### Mandatory Nodes (Priority Order)
1. **Spreading Darkness** — guarantees **4 Ritual altars per map** *(take first, non-negotiable)*
2. **He Approaches** — increases monster rarity → more Tribute + loot
3. **Ominous Portents** — raises Omen drop chance significantly
4. **Reinvigorated Sacrifices** — removes the revive Tribute penalty (+28% Tribute per map)
5. **Tempting Offers** — more reward choices per altar
6. **Reduced Reroll Cost** — stack until rerolls are free

### Additional Nodes
- **Summoning Circle (Overlord's Domain)** — spawns bosses during Ritual → boss rewards at all altars → massive Tribute generation
- **Prayer and Pledge** — 10% of Tribute spent counts toward King's Audience progress
- **Traveller's Woe** — guarantees specific Ritual types (target-farm specific hazard types)
- **Traveller's Woe: Tainted** — increases belt/charm/flask appearance rates (great for Headhunter/Mageblood hunting)

### Avoid
- Item Rarity investment — not worth it for Ritual; pack size and rare monsters generate far more Tribute

## Tablet Strategy

### Basic Setup (Low Budget)
- 1× **Freedom of Faith Ritual Precursor Tablet** — doubles altars
- 2× common Ritual Tablets with flexible mods
- Target Tier 16 maps with area level bonuses

### Advanced Setup
- Run **city maps** for higher pack sizes
- 4× Ritual Tablets including:
  - 2× with **"+3 additional reroll Favours"**
  - 2× with **"Rerolling Favours costs 20–30% less Tribute"**
- Stack enough reroll cost reduction to make rerolls effectively **free**

> 💡 Always verify current prices on [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja) before selling — Omen prices shift daily

### Defer Strategy
- Defer any item worth more than 3 Divines if you can't afford it now
- 6% discount per defer compounds quickly on expensive items
- Defer **An Audience with the King** if Tribute is low — it will return cheaper

## Head of the King Chain — Advanced Strat

Once you've fought the **King in the Mists** and obtained **The Head of the King**:

1. Use it to convert a map into **Rite of the Nameless**
2. Chain up to **6 maps in sequence** — enemies and bosses carry over between maps
3. Each map in the chain provides a buff to all subsequent maps
4. Final map reward: massive Tribute + boss loot explosion
5. Pairs with **Blood on the Stones** Atlas node for Fracturing Orb drops (3.5–5 Divines each)

> This is the highest-ceiling Ritual play but requires completing the King questline first
