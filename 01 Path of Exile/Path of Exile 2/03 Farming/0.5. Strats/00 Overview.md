## General Farming Philosophy

- **Early league (Days 1–3):** Expedition or Ritual. Both are accessible immediately and don't require heavy investment.
- **Mid league (Days 3–7):** Breach peaks here as jewelry demand rises. Run alongside your primary mechanic.
- **Late league (Week 2+):** Delirium and Temple become relevant as players chase jewel and quality crafting.
- **Currency trees in Breach:** Never prioritize early — jewelry always outvalues currency drops at league start.
- **Avoid Delirium week 1** — demand for Liquid Emotions and jewel crafting is near zero.

## Atlas Foundation (Do This First)

Before specializing, set this generic base regardless of your chosen mechanic:

1. **Push Corrupted Nexuses** → Atlas Passive Points ASAP
2. **Generic loot multipliers first** (rare monsters, pack size, waystone quantity) — these compound every mechanic
3. **Slot Tablets on every Tower** — even weak tablets outperform bare maps significantly
4. **Unlock the Burning Monolith path** (Tier 10+) for access to endgame content
5. **Pick your master** aligned with your mechanic:
   - Expedition → **Jado's Spycraft**
   - Ritual/Corrupted → **Doryani's Science**
   - Boss hunts → **Hilda's Hunting**

### Biome Selection

| Biome | Best For |
| --- | --- |
| **Grass** | General farming — best all-rounder |
| **Desert** | High density — great for Breach |
| **Forest** | Rare monster focus |
| **Mountain** | Ritual + Pack size builds |

**Recommended base:** Grass + Forest + Desert for stable daily farming across most strategies.

### Universal Atlas Nodes (Take Before Specializing)

- **Pathkeepers** — +15% waystone quantity from bosses
- **Valuable Paths** — +10% waystone quantity
- **Top of the World** — precursor reveal + 10% waystone chance
- **Rising Danger / Twin Threats / No Simple Battles** — rare monster density
- **Archaeological Interest** — +15% tablet quantity

---
## Recommended Progression Path

1. Campaign → Cruel → Atlas entry
2. Grab generic rare monster + waystone nodes on Atlas tree
3. Clear first Corrupted Nexus → bank Atlas points
4. **Days 1–2:** Run Expedition for raw currency + gear
5. **Days 2–3:** Layer in Ritual for Omens and unique items
6. **Day 3+:** Transition or add Breach for jewelry income
7. **Week 2:** Evaluate Delirium/Abyss based on market prices on [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja)
