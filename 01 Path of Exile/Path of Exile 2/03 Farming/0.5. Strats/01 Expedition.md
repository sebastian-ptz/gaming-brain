## Why Expedition

- **S-Tier** — best early league currency printer in 0.5
- Provides **gear, currency, Runes, and Alloys** all from the same mechanic
- Vendors let you **buy items directly with stashed currency** — bypasses crafting nerfs
- Zero tablet investment needed to get started
- Works well even on undergeared characters once you learn encounter management

> ⚠️ Encounters are **dangerous** for low-level/undergeared characters — learn to run away from bad remnant combos

---
## How It Works

1. Find an **Expedition encounter** on your map (flagpoles + explosive markers)
2. Place **5 explosive charges** chaining from each other
3. Prioritize:
   - **Runic Monster Markers** → rare enemies → Logbooks + high loot
   - **Excavated Chest Markers** → chests with artifacts + currency
   - **Remnants** → modifiers on the next explosion (good and bad)
4. Detonate → clear spawned monsters immediately
5. Collect **Artifacts** and sell to Expedition vendors for currency and items

### Remnant Strategy
- ✅ **Take:** Pack size, rarity, quantity buffs
- ❌ **Avoid:** Enemy immunities, guaranteed crits, cannot be stunned
- Use **Shift+V** to preview placements before committing

## Atlas Setup

### Master
**Jado's Spycraft** — unlocked by completing a Deadly Map Boss. Pick the node:
- **Veressium Remnants have 10% chance to be rolled an additional time** (core Expedition node)

### Atlas Passive Priorities
1. **Detailed Records** — more Logbooks + guaranteed 3 implicit modifiers (most important)
2. **Legendary Battles** — more rare monsters + Exotic Coinage drops
3. **Disturbed Rest** — +50% Runic Monster Markers

### Generic Nodes to Take First
- Rare monster density nodes (Rising Danger, Twin Threats)
- Waystone quantity nodes (Pathkeepers, Valuable Paths)
- Pack size nodes

## The Grand Expedition Strat

The premium Expedition strategy in 0.5:

1. Push to **Tier 8+ maps**
2. Hunt for the **Mirror of the Fallen Skies** unique map
3. When found — take the **Aldur's Saga** reward option
4. This modifies the next **Grand Expedition** to spawn with "at least 5/6/7 slots"
5. **5-slot minimum** Grand Expeditions average **2+ Divines per map**
6. Peak runs can hit **9 Divines per map**

> 💡 Players have reported **100+ Divines in ~1.5 days** with zero tablet investment using this strat

---
## Logbook Strategy

Logbooks are the premium sub-content of Expedition:

- Grants **20 explosive charges** instead of 5
- Chance to encounter **Olroth** — the Expedition Pinnacle Boss
- Use Logbooks on a **juiced Atlas** for best results

**Best Logbook Areas:**
- Ocean / coastal Logbooks for max density

## What to Buy From Vendors

In 0.5 vendors stock buyable items using your Expedition artifacts. Priority purchases:

1. **Gear upgrades** — use artifacts to skip crafting entirely early league
2. **Exotic Coinage** — refresh vendor stock for better items
3. **Runes and Alloys** — sell on trade for raw Divines
