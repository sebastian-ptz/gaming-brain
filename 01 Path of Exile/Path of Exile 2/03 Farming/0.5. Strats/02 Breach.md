## Why Breach

- **S-Tier** — scales extremely rapidly by Day 2-3
- **Dual-value farm:** target jewelry from Genesis Tree + natural Breach loot density
- Breach monsters have some of the **highest base loot drop rates** in the game
- High-roll jewelry and skill-boosting amulets sell for **multiple Divines** each
- Compounds with general rare monster nodes

> ⚠️ Slightly weaker than Expedition on Day 1 — prioritize Expedition first, then layer Breach in by Day 2

---
## How It Works

1. Activate a **Breach Hand** in your map → Hiveborn enemies start spawning
2. **Dual timer system:**
   - Dark purple bar — base timer that decays naturally
   - Light purple bar — extended by kills (keep killing to extend)
3. Fill the Breach → elite enemies spawn → defeat them to **collapse** the Breach
4. Collect **Breach Splinters** → 300 = 1 Breachstone → Realmgate → **Xesht** Pinnacle Boss

### Encounter Types
| Type | What It Is |
| --- | --- |
| **Breach Hives** | Defense events — protect Ailith from waves |
| **Breach Fortresses** | Larger encounters with maze escape phases |
| **Hive Colonies** | Advanced strongholds with Breachlord bosses (Tul, Esh) |

---
## Genesis Tree — The Key Mechanic

The Genesis Tree is the 0.5 Breach crafting system for **target-farming exclusive jewelry**:

- Collect **Hiveblood** and **Wombgifts** from Breach encounters
- Feed them into the Genesis Tree to craft unique jewelry with **guaranteed modifiers**
- Output: **rings, amulets, belts** with premium stats
- Early league demand for high-roll jewelry is extremely high → multiple Divines per piece

> 💡 The Genesis Tree is what separates Breach from other mechanics — it's not just loot, it's a crafting engine for some of the best early-league gear

## Atlas Setup

### Atlas Passive Priorities
1. **Frantic Invasion** — +100% Vruun spawn rate (core density node — take first)
2. **Reality Wound** — +10 seconds to Breach collapse window (massive QoL)
3. **Wombgift / Hiveblood Quantity** — +5% each (more Genesis Tree fuel)
4. **Sole Purpose** — Ailith skill specialization

### Tablet Strategy
- Slot **Breach Tablets** on towers to add Breach encounters to nearby maps
- Stack with **pack size** and **rare monster** mods on Precursor Tablets for more Hiveborn density

### Biome
**Desert** — best density for Breach (rare monsters + effectiveness)

---
## What NOT to Do

> ❌ **Never prioritize the currency tree in Breach early league**

The currency tree drops are nearly worthless in the first week. The jewelry from Genesis Tree consistently sells for **multiple Divines** while currency tree drops are fractions of that. Switch to currency tree only in late league when jewelry demand cools.
