## Why Abyss

- **B-Tier** — outclassed early by Expedition and Breach but has stable consistent value
- Drops **core crafting materials** (Jawbones, Ribs, Collarbones) that are always needed for endgame crafting
- Good fallback if Expedition output underperforms for your build
- Skip Days 1–2, then layer in by Day 3+

> ⚠️ Encounters are **notoriously dangerous** — dense, tough monster packs with sudden damage spikes. Don't prioritize this early on a fragile build.

---
## How It Works

1. Find **Fissures** on your map → they spawn monster packs and rares
2. Clearing Fissure monsters triggers an **Abyssal Pit** — Abyssal Invaders spawn
3. Sealing the Pit drops an **Abyssal Armory** (high-tier loot cache)
4. Defeated monsters drop **Gnawed Jawbones** — add hidden modifiers to equipment
5. Some Pits reveal underground entrances to **Abyssal Depths**

### Abyssal Depths
- An undead city with rare enemies carrying Abyssal modifiers
- Contains **4 Ancient Spires** each with a boss → new Unique items
- Rewards: Lineage Support Gems, Omen of Abyssal Echoes, Ulaman's Gaze rune

### Well of Souls
- Take Abyssal-infused gear (or Waystones) to the Well of Souls
- Reveal hidden modifiers added by Gnawed Jawbones
- Choose 1 of 3 mod options

## Atlas Setup

### Atlas Passive Priorities

**Path A — Rarity / Ulaman currency focus:**
- Rarity nodes first
- Ulaman's drop rate nodes

**Path B — Effectiveness / Amanamu focus:**
- Effectiveness nodes
- Monster density nodes

### Core Nodes
- **Lord of the Pit** — key Abyss scaling node
- **Wild Treasure** — more Abyssal Armory rewards
- **Balance of Power** — take "Any Faction" option only *(bugged if you pick a specific faction)*
- **Ruiner's Desires** — more Abyssal depth access

### Generic Support Nodes
- **Divine Blessing / Vivid Spirits / Purified** — shrines + spirits for density
- **Blood on Stone** — boss rewards
- **Well-Equipped Opponents / Evolving Thorns** — rare monster buffs

### Master
**Jado's Spycraft** with **Partial Translations** — doubles tablet effects (great for Abyss Tablets)

## Low Budget Abyss Strat

**Tablet Setup (~180 Exalts investment):**
- 1× Elevated Tablet (+1 zone level)
- 1× Abyss Tablet (mandatory additional Abyss encounter)
- 1× Overseer Tablet (boss empowerment)

**Map Rolling:**
- 6-modifier Waystones with 30–40% item rarity
- Drop to 5-mod if the mods are too dangerous

**Atlas:**
- Shrines/Spirits: Divine Blessing, Vivid Spirits, Purified, Favor the Villain
- Abyss: Lord of the Pit, Wild Treasure, Balance of Power (Any Faction), Ruiner's Desires
- Boss/Monsters: Blood on Stone, Well-Equipped Opponents, Evolving Thorns
