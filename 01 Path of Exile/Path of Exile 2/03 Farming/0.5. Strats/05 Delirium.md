## Why Delirium (and Why to Skip Early)

- **B-Tier** — produces Liquid Emotions for jewel crafting and amulet anointments
- Zero early-league demand: players can't afford jewel crafting or anointments in week 1
- **Skip entirely for the first week** — invest elsewhere and return when the economy matures
- Peaks when the league economy is established and players are optimizing builds

---
## How It Works

1. Walk through a **Delirium Mirror** on the map → engulfs the area in spreading fog
2. Stay inside the fog — stepping out starts a countdown to end the encounter
3. Hit **Fractured Mirror Shards** along the fog path:

| Shard Type | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 🔵 **Blue** | Standard enemy pack |
| 🟣 **Purple** | Empowered enemies — better loot |
| 🟡 **Gold** | Spawns a Delirium-exclusive boss |
| 🔴 **Red** | Teleports to **Loathsome Mire** arena |

4. **Deliriousness** stacks across chained maps → higher difficulty AND better rewards
5. First chained map starts at **10% Deliriousness** and carries over increasing with each map

### Key Tip
> Use the **Progress Bar** to locate the Delirium boss before the timer expires — reaching the boss area freezes the bar, removing time pressure entirely.

### Boss Encounters
| Boss | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Omniphobia, Fear Manifest** | Tier 11+ maps |
| **Kosis, the Revelation** | Tier 11+ maps (harder) |
| **Tangmazu, the Raven Trickster** | Strange Reflections questline |

## Atlas Setup

### Atlas Passive Priorities
1. **Come on! Face me!** — +25% unique boss shard chance; killing uniques pauses the timer 10s
2. **You can't scare me anymore!** — unique monsters drop Potent Emotions
3. **Grand Mirror Chance** — extra shards + Grand Mirrors
4. **Recurring Nightmares** — better fog spread across multi-map chains

### Tablet Strategy
- **Delirium Tablets** slot into Lost Towers to add Delirium nodes to nearby maps
- Essential for sustaining Delirium farming — drop from Delirium events and are tradeable
- Stack Delirium Tablets with pack size mods on Precursor Tablets

### Biome
**Boss** — concentrate loot onto boss kills for Liquid Emotion efficiency

## Simulacrum

The Delirium pinnacle content:
- Collect **300 Simulacrum Splinters** (drop from Delirium enemies) = 1 **Simulacrum**
- Enter via Realmgate with the Simulacrum item
- Clear **15 waves** of escalating Delirium content
- Reward: **+2 Atlas Passive Points** for the Delirium tree
- Very difficult — requires a strong, well-geared build
